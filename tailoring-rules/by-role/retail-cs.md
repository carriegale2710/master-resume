# **No-bullshit CV checklist specific by the role (Retail / Customer Service)**

> Tailor your CV for the specific retail or customer service role you are applying for.
> You MUST check CV follows every-resume.md first before applying the role-specific guidelines below.
> Repo rules win: numbers only from `master/metrics-log.csv` (use its "Safe wording"), no NDA-covered names, Australian English spelling, never invent a figure.

## **1\. Retail Assistant / Sales Assistant**

**Headline example:** “Retail Sales Assistant | Customer Service, POS, Stock Replenishment”

### **Must‑have signals**

- **Customer-facing focus is clear**: serving customers, sales, stock, store standards.
- **Tools named as in the posting**: POS/EFTPOS, stocktake or inventory systems, loyalty programs (only if the master supports them).
- **Reliability signals**: shifts covered, opening/closing duties, cash handling, trusted with responsibility.

### **Experience bullets**

- **What**: role, store type, customer volume, duties you owned.
- **How**:
  - Serving and upselling (product knowledge, add-on recommendations)
  - Processing transactions and handling cash accurately
  - Replenishing stock, merchandising, keeping displays to standard
  - Handling returns, complaints and difficult customers calmly
- **Why (impact)**, only where the metrics log supports it:
  - “Exceeded weekly sales target” (state the figure only if logged)
  - “Reduced till discrepancies”
  - “Handled X customers per shift”
- Show **teamwork**: handovers between shifts, training new staff, covering gaps.

### **Formatting specifics**

- One page, single column, Calibri. Availability and work rights on one line using `${RESUME_WORKRIGHTS}`.
- Entry-level or graduate role: Education & Training under the summary, otherwise at the bottom.

## **2\. Customer Service Representative (phone / chat / email / in-person)**

**Headline example:** “Customer Service Representative | Complaint Resolution, CRM, Phone & Chat Support”

### **Must‑have signals**

- **Channel is explicit**: phone, live chat, email, in-person, or a mix.
- **Resolution focus**: first-contact resolution, escalation handling, follow-through.
- **Systems named as in the posting**: CRM, ticketing, knowledge base (only if the master supports them).

### **Experience bullets**

- **What**: type of customers, channels, issues handled, volume.
- **How**:
  - Diagnosing the problem, explaining options in plain language
  - Logging and tracking cases in a CRM or ticketing system
  - De-escalating upset customers and escalating correctly
  - Following policy and privacy requirements
- **Why (impact)**, only where the metrics log supports it:
  - “Resolved X% of queries on first contact”
  - “Maintained satisfaction score of X”
  - “Reduced average handling or response time”
- Show **situations, not adjectives**: one short example of a hard call or complaint beats “excellent communicator”.

### **Formatting specifics**

- Same layout as above: one page (two maximum), single column.
- Mirror the posting’s keywords (e.g. “first contact resolution”, “omnichannel”) only where the master supports them.
