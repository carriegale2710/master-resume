# **CV checklist: Design (role-specific only)**

> Check `every-resume.md` first (headline, what+how+why bullets, no Skills section, dates, links). Only role-specific ticks are below.
> Repo rules win: figures only from `master/metrics-log.csv` (examples are illustrative; otherwise describe the feedback or decision), no NDA-covered names, never invent a project, tool or result.
> CV stays text‑first, single column, no images; the portfolio does the visual work. Portfolio link is prominent. Tools sit inside bullets, only where the master supports them.

## **Pick one role and headline it**

The posting's wording tells you which. Do not blend them.

| Role            | Core question                                | Owns                                                                 | Lead with                                    |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Product Designer | Does it solve the right problem?            | Research, concepts, UI, handoff, outcomes                            | 2–3 full process case studies with outcomes  |
| Service Designer | How does the whole service run?             | Journeys, blueprints, touchpoints, ops, policy                       | Journey map and blueprint, co‑design         |
| UX/UI Designer  | How does it work _and_ look?                 | Flows, wireframes, interface, prototypes                             | End‑to‑end case study, flows to final UI     |
| UI Designer     | How does the interface look and behave?      | Components, layout, type, colour, design system, states              | Polished screens, component library, handoff |
| Visual Designer | How does it communicate?                     | Brand, graphics, illustration, layout, campaign assets               | Brand and visual work, craft, consistency    |
| UX Researcher   | What do users need, and what's the evidence? | Research plans, interviews, surveys, usability tests, synthesis      | Method choice, insights, decisions changed   |

## **All design roles**

- [ ] 2–3 case studies or projects, each stating problem/brief, users, constraints and your role versus the team in plain words.
- [ ] Process shown (sketches, wireframes, iterations), not just final screens.
- [ ] Collaboration shown (PMs, engineers, stakeholders).
- [ ] Redact NDA‑covered client details and participant identities; only work you made or clearly credited.

## **Product Designer (Junior)**

**Headline example:** "Junior Product Designer | UX Research, Interaction Design, Figma"

- [ ] Research, concepts, UI and handoff (specs, components) all shown; at least one interactive prototype link or walkthrough.
- [ ] Research methods and key insights named (interviews, surveys, usability tests).
- [ ] Internships emphasise research, flows, UI, design systems and usability testing.

## **Service Designer (Junior)**

**Headline example:** "Junior Service Designer | Journey Mapping, Blueprinting, Co‑Design"

- [ ] Stakeholder mapping, journey maps, service blueprints and a co‑design workshop (artefacts, no identifiable participants).
- [ ] Frontstage and backstage shown, covering digital and offline channels, policy and org constraints.
- [ ] Implementation or pilot evidence, or say plainly it was a concept.
- [ ] Experience highlights cross‑functional, operations, policy or customer service work.

## **UX/UI Designer (Junior)**

**Headline example:** "Junior UX/UI Designer | User Flows, Wireframing, Prototyping, Figma"

- [ ] Both halves shown: UX (flows, structure, usability) and UI (visual interface), problem to final screens.
- [ ] Less strategy and metrics than Product Designer, more flows and interface.
- [ ] At least one before‑and‑after flow and one clickable prototype; platforms match the posting.

## **UI Designer (Junior)**

**Headline example:** "Junior UI Designer | Visual Interface Design, Design Systems, Figma"

- [ ] Interface craft is the focus: layout, type, colour, spacing, hierarchy, states (empty, error, loading, hover).
- [ ] Component or design‑system work (components, tokens, variants), with a component sheet or mini design system.
- [ ] Responsive layouts and a visible accessibility consideration (contrast, tap targets, text size).
- [ ] A handoff example (specs, annotations, dev‑ready file); motion samples optional.
- [ ] No research or strategy claimed that was not done.

## **Visual Designer (Junior)**

**Headline example:** "Junior Visual Designer | Brand Identity, Layout, Illustration, Adobe Creative Suite"

- [ ] Brand and communication focus (identity, campaigns, social, print, illustration, motion), with typography, colour and composition consistent across assets.
- [ ] At least one full brand or campaign project: brief, process, applications (mockups, formats, sizes).
- [ ] Range within one consistent system, not unrelated one‑offs.
- [ ] Tools named as in the posting (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, Figma), only if the master supports them.
- [ ] No stock or client assets you cannot show.

## **UX Researcher (Junior)**

**Headline example:** "Junior UX Researcher | Usability Testing, User Interviews, Survey Design, Synthesis"

- [ ] Method range with reasons (interviews, surveys, usability tests, diary studies); one qualitative and one quantitative method, or a stated reason why not.
- [ ] Evidence to decision: what was found and what changed.
- [ ] Rigour in plain language: sample, recruitment, bias, ethics and consent.
- [ ] Redacted artefacts (discussion guide, survey, affinity map, journey map, insight report) and a readout written for non‑researchers.
- [ ] No participant names or recordings.
