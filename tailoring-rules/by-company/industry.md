# **CV checklist: Tailoring by industry**

> Use this with a role checklist (SWE, design, retail/CS, graduate, startup). It changes the vocabulary, proof and risks for the field, not the facts.
> You MUST check CV follows every-resume.md first.
> Repo rules win: numbers only from `master/metrics-log.csv` (use its "Safe wording"), no NDA-covered names, Australian English spelling, never invent a skill, tool, date, licence, clearance or figure. Mirror a posting's terms only where the master supports them.

## **How to use**

1. Pick the industry from the posting, the company's site and its customers.
2. Apply that section's vocabulary, proof and cautions to the headline and bullets.
3. Flag anything the industry expects that the master doesn't evidence (as a `TODO`, never fill it in).

---

## **1\. Finance / Banking / Insurance**

- **Cares about**: accuracy, risk, compliance, auditability, security, customer trust.
- **Vocabulary** (only if evidenced): reconciliation, KYC/AML, payments, controls, reporting, regulated environment.
- **Proof to lead with**: accuracy, attention to detail, handling sensitive data, process adherence, error reduction.
- **Bullet angle**: what you did carefully and the risk or cost it avoided.
- **Cautions**: don't claim licences or regulatory knowledge you lack; check right‑to‑work and citizenship requirements in the posting.

## **2\. SaaS / Software products**

- **Cares about**: shipping, users, retention, reliability, iteration speed.
- **Vocabulary** (only if evidenced): onboarding, activation, churn, roadmap, API, release, B2B.
- **Proof to lead with**: shipped features, user feedback loops, tooling and automation, product thinking.
- **Bullet angle**: feature → how built → user or business outcome.
- **Cautions**: don't use growth metrics unless logged; use "Safe wording" for estimates.

## **3\. Communications / Marketing / Media**

- **Cares about**: audience, message, channel, brand voice, deadlines, results.
- **Vocabulary** (only if evidenced): campaign, content calendar, copywriting, social, SEO, stakeholder comms, analytics.
- **Proof to lead with**: published or delivered work, audience reached, tone adaptation, turnaround under deadline.
- **Bullet angle**: audience + channel + what you produced + response.
- **Cautions**: engagement and reach figures only from the metrics log; attribute team work fairly.

## **4\. Health / Medical / Community care**

- **Cares about**: patient or client safety, privacy, empathy, procedure, documentation.
- **Vocabulary** (only if evidenced): clinical, client‑centred, privacy and confidentiality, handover, documentation, compliance.
- **Proof to lead with**: reliability, calm under pressure, accurate records, working with vulnerable people, teamwork.
- **Bullet angle**: situation → careful action → safe or positive outcome.
- **Cautions**: never claim registration, clinical qualifications, checks (e.g. working with children, police) or training the master doesn't list; no identifiable patient or client details.

## **5\. Science / Research / Laboratory**

- **Cares about**: method, rigour, reproducibility, data quality, safety.
- **Vocabulary** (only if evidenced): experimental design, protocol, data analysis, statistics, lab safety, publication, reporting.
- **Proof to lead with**: methods and instruments used, data handled, results communicated, safe and accurate practice.
- **Bullet angle**: question → method → finding or contribution.
- **Cautions**: no inflated authorship; name publications only if real and in the master.

## **6\. Education / Training**

- **Cares about**: learner outcomes, communication, planning, inclusion, safeguarding.
- **Proof to lead with**: teaching, tutoring, mentoring or onboarding others, materials created, learner progress.
- **Cautions**: no registration or checks claimed unless in the master.

## **7\. Government / Public sector / Non‑profit**

- **Cares about**: public value, process, accountability, equity, selection criteria.
- **Proof to lead with**: evidence against each stated criterion, policy or procedure adherence, stakeholder work.
- **Bullet angle**: mirror selection criteria wording using what the master supports.
- **Cautions**: eligibility (citizenship, clearance) is stated as in the posting; use `${RESUME_WORKRIGHTS}` and don't claim clearances.

## **8\. Retail / Hospitality / Customer‑facing**

- **Cares about**: service, reliability, speed, teamwork, shift flexibility.
- **Proof to lead with**: customer volume, accuracy under pressure, handling complaints.
- **See**: `retail-cs-resume-checklist.md` for role detail.

## **9\. Engineering / Trades / Logistics / Manufacturing**

- **Cares about**: safety, process, quality, uptime, compliance, efficiency.
- **Vocabulary** (only if evidenced): standards, inspection, maintenance, supply chain, WHS.
- **Proof to lead with**: safe practice, quality and output, equipment and systems used.
- **Cautions**: licences and tickets only if listed in the master.
