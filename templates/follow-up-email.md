# Follow-up emails

## 1. After applying

**Subject:** Re: [original subject, or "Application – \<role\>"]

Hi [first name],

I applied for the [role] on [day/month] and wanted to confirm you received my application. [One sentence on why you are a fit: strongest relevant project + outcome.]

Is there anything else I can provide?

Thanks,
${RESUME_NAME}
${RESUME_EMAIL}

## 2. After an interview

**Subject:** Thank you – [role] interview

Hi [first name],

Thank you for your time on [day]. [One sentence on something specific discussed, e.g. \<topic\> on \<team\>.] [One sentence tying it to a relevant project or skill from the master.]

I'm keen on the role and happy to send anything further.

Thanks,
${RESUME_NAME}
${RESUME_EMAIL}

## 3. After a cold email with no reply

**Subject:** Re: [original subject]

Hi [first name],

Following up in case my note got buried. [One sentence restating the ask, shorter than before.] If it's not a good time, no problem.

Thanks,
${RESUME_NAME}
${RESUME_EMAIL}

## 4. After a rejection (optional)

**Subject:** Re: [role]

Hi [first name],

Thank you for letting me know. I enjoyed learning about [team or project]. If a suitable role opens, I'd welcome the chance to be considered, and any feedback on my application would be useful.

Thanks,
${RESUME_NAME}

## Before sending

- [ ] Right template for the stage and timing
- [ ] Name and role spelled correctly
- [ ] One ask only
- [ ] Every claim traces to the master
- [ ] Under 100 words
