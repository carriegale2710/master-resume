# **Version A: Corporate / Graduate Program**

**Associate Software Engineer — Graduate Program**

## **About the Role**

Join a structured graduate program where you'll build foundational engineering skills while contributing to real, production systems. You'll be embedded within an established team, learning our practices, tools, and delivery standards alongside experienced developers, with a clear pathway toward AI-native engineering as your capability grows.

## **What You'll Do**

- Complete regular tasks and tickets promptly and to a high standard, delivering progress frequently
- Write clean, well-tested code and include meaningful tests before raising a pull request
- Participate in code reviews, scrum ceremonies, and team retrospectives — providing and challenging estimates
- Contribute to technical documentation and the team's knowledge base
- Collaborate with technical and non-technical stakeholders to translate requirements into working solutions
- Investigate technical issues and contribute to root-cause analysis
- Progressively engage with AI-assisted development — prompt engineering, structured outputs, and evaluation — as your foundations solidify

## **What You Bring**

- A degree (completed or final year) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical discipline
- Foundational programming ability in at least one of Java, Python, or JavaScript/TypeScript
- Understanding of software engineering principles, including Git and version control
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proactive, curious mindset
- Ability to take constructive feedback and act on it, reducing rework over time
- Excellent written and verbal communication, and comfort working with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Eligibility to work in Australia (citizenship, permanent residency, or full working rights)

## **Nice to Have**

- Exposure to machine learning, generative AI, or large language models
- Personal, academic, or open-source projects demonstrating genuine interest in software engineering
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)

## **Why Join**

- Structured learning & development program with coaching and mentoring from senior/principal engineers
- Defined governance, clear reporting lines, and predictable delivery cadence
- Leave benefits, wellbeing resources, and on-site perks
- A supportive environment to build the habits of a strong engineer before specializing

_Full-time, on-site, 5 days/week. Reports to a team lead within an established engineering squad._
