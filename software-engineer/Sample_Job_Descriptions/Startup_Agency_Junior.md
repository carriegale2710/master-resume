# **Version B: Small Team / Agency / Startup**

**Full-Stack Engineer — Founding/Early Engineering Team**

## **About the Role**

We're a small team shipping fast, and we need someone who wants to own problems end-to-end — not just implement designs or specs handed down from above. You'll be given context on _why_ something matters and the freedom to design and ship the solution yourself. There's no hierarchy of who's allowed to touch what: if you find a bug, fix it; if you have an idea, raise it.

## **What You'll Do**

- Ship complete features across the stack — frontend, backend, database — not just your specialism
- Build with AI as standard practice: scaffolding, troubleshooting, and accelerating iteration, with the judgment to know when not to trust the output
- Move fast without much process — perfect systems aren't the goal, shipping good ones is
- Work directly with founders, designers, or a small product squad rather than through layers of approval
- Take ownership of a problem from first principles through to production and follow-up after deploy
- Wear multiple hats as needed — the team is small, and boundaries between roles are loose

## **What You Bring**

- Solid working proficiency in a modern stack (e.g., TypeScript, React/Next.js/Svelte, or a typed backend language) — comfort picking up unfamiliar parts of the stack matters more than deep expertise in any one framework
- 1–3 years of professional experience, or equivalent demonstrable project work (side projects, hackathons, open source)
- AI-native workflow — you use AI coding assistants daily and can sanity-check what they produce
- Ownership mindset: you ask questions early, test thoroughly, and don't wait to be told what's next
- Comfort with ambiguity — you help define the plan rather than waiting for a complete brief
- Clean, maintainable commits and clear PR descriptions; you write code the next person can read

## **Nice to Have**

- Startup or small-team experience where you've shipped in fast-moving, ambiguous environments
- Experience with the specific stack pieces you haven't used yet — we'll help you ramp
- Interest in exploring how AI can differentiate the product, not just accelerate its build

## **Why Join**

- Your work will be seen and felt — small team, direct impact
- Flexibility in how and where you work, as long as you deliver
- No exec gatekeeping, no multi-week approval cycles
- Room to grow fast into new areas as the team scales

_Flexible/remote-friendly. Reports directly to founders or a small squad lead._
