# Master resume: purpose, best practice, maintenance

Researched 2026-10-29. Sources are career-service and career-advice pages. No official standard exists for a master resume, so treat this as consensus practice, not specification.

## What it is for

- A private, complete record of your career that you draw from to build each tailored resume. You do not submit it. ([CPA Australia, In the Black](https://intheblack.cpaaustralia.com.au/careers-and-workplace/why-master-resume-will-help-career); [Monster](https://www.monster.com/career-advice/resume/master-resume))
- It stops you forgetting project outcomes and skills as your career moves on. CPA Australia's source calls it a "personal filing cabinet", and it also helps you recall specific examples for interviews. ([CPA Australia](https://intheblack.cpaaustralia.com.au/careers-and-workplace/why-master-resume-will-help-career))
- Length does not matter in the master. It matters only in the tailored version, which is typically one to two pages. ([Alberta ALIS](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/how-to-create-your-master-resume/); [University of Denver](https://career.du.edu/?p=26508), as summarised by search; page not read directly)
- Not everyone uses one. Monster notes some people write each resume from scratch to force sharper tailoring. ([Monster](https://www.monster.com/career-advice/resume/master-resume))

## What to put in it

Alberta ALIS gives the most concrete checklist ([source](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/how-to-create-your-master-resume/)):

- All work-like experience: jobs, internships, volunteering, committees. Record organisation, title, dates, duties, skills, tools, awards, achievements, and numbers where you can.
- All training and education: institution, dates, credential, standout marks, skills gained.
- Gaps explained, since caregiving, travel and temporary work can still show skills.
- Languages, memberships and other distinguishing qualities.
- Ask friends and co-workers about your strengths. They may notice things you missed.

CPA Australia adds project results, budget size, software implemented and training completed. ([source](https://intheblack.cpaaustralia.com.au/careers-and-workplace/why-master-resume-will-help-career))

## How to tailor from it

1. Compare the posting's requirements with the master and select the best-matching experiences. ([Alberta ALIS](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/how-to-create-your-master-resume/))
2. Put the most relevant achievements first and cut to one or two pages. (same source)
3. Mirror the posting's keywords only where they truthfully fit. (same source)
4. Keep your core story consistent with LinkedIn. Many different CVs can confuse recruiters, so tailor language more than facts. ([CPA Australia](https://intheblack.cpaaustralia.com.au/careers-and-workplace/why-master-resume-will-help-career))
5. Proofread when copying into the tailored copy. ([Alberta ALIS](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/how-to-create-your-master-resume/))

For Australian applicants: one page is the usual target for graduates and early-career, two for experienced candidates, though some sources say longer. ([SEEK](https://www.seek.com.au/career-advice/article/resume-cv); [Macquarie University](https://students.mq.edu.au/careers/applying/resumes-cvs))

## How to maintain it

- Update whenever you learn a skill, finish a course or complete a project. ([Alberta ALIS](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/how-to-create-your-master-resume/))
- Also set a recurring review tied to your performance-review cycle (quarterly, six-monthly or annual). ([CPA Australia](https://intheblack.cpaaustralia.com.au/careers-and-workplace/why-master-resume-will-help-career))
- Record raw wins as they happen. You can cut detail later, but you cannot easily recover a forgotten fact. (Common advice across [Monster](https://www.monster.com/career-advice/resume/master-resume) and [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/post/how-to-create-a-master-resume); not verified by direct fetch.)
- Edit only the master. Tailored copies are disposable. (Inference from the sources above, and this repo's rule.)

## Comparison with this repo

The repo already follows the consensus: master as sole source of facts, tailored copies in `applications/`, a metrics log, and a `TODO` convention for unknowns.

Gaps against the sources:

- No scheduled review. Add a recurring check, such as monthly or after each application, that clears `TODO` items, the unverified-number flags and the skills-inventory R/F/O markers.
- Skills inventory markers (R/F/O) are still unset in `master/master-resume.md` section 1.
- No "ask others for strengths" step to surface missed items.
