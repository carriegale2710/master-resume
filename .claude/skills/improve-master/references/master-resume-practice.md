# Master resume practice

Distilled from `docs/research/2026-10-29-master-resume.md`. Consensus from career-service pages, not a standard. Sources: [Alberta ALIS](https://alis.alberta.ca/look-for-work/resumes-and-references/how-to-create-your-master-resume/) and [CPA Australia](https://intheblack.cpaaustralia.com.au/careers-and-workplace/why-master-resume-will-help-career) were read in full. Other claims below marked (summary) came from search summaries only.

## Purpose

- A private, complete record. Never submitted. Tailored copies are cut from it.
- Length is irrelevant in the master. It matters only in the tailored copy (one page for graduate and early-career in Australia).
- It also serves as a recall aid for interview examples.

## What a complete entry holds

Per role, project, course or volunteer item:

- Organisation, title, dates, team size.
- Duties, tools, skills used.
- Outcomes with numbers where real, and the evidence for each number.
- Awards and recognition.
- Links.

Also: gaps in dates explained, languages and memberships, and strengths other people notice.

## Maintenance rules

1. Update when something is learnt, finished or shipped, not later. Raw wins can be trimmed afterwards, but a forgotten fact is hard to recover (summary).
2. Hold a scheduled review as well. CPA Australia ties it to the performance-review cycle. This repo uses section 9 of the master (weekly, monthly, quarterly).
3. Edit only the master. Tailored copies are disposable.
4. Retire material to the archive. Do not delete it.
5. Keep one core story. Dates, titles and levels must agree across the master, LinkedIn and every tailored copy.

## Quality bar for a bullet

- What was done, how, and why it mattered. Past tense, plain words.
- A number only if it has a metrics-log row. Otherwise use the safe wording.
- No filler adjectives. Australian English.

## Gaps this repo should check on every review

- Any `TODO` field.
- Metrics-log rows typed Estimate, Projection, Self-reported, Unverified, Conflict or Unquantified, and any TODO evidence cell.
- Items in the "Conflicts between this master and older CVs" list.
- Skills-inventory level placements unconfirmed, and skills with no evidencing bullet.
- Interview stories (STAR) still `TODO` in the private notes.
- Section 9 checklist items overdue.
- Recent work not yet in the master.

## Limits

- No official standard exists. Most sources are commercial career-advice pages.
- Monster notes some people write each resume from scratch instead. That is a valid alternative, not the repo's choice.
